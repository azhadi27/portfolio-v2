# SECTION 1 — ATTITUDE & MINDSET (20 MARKS)

These questions filter **coachability, accountability, ownership**.

---

## Q1. Tell us about a time something broke or failed in your work. What did you do?

### Strong answer indicators:

Candidate:

✅ takes ownership
✅ explains investigation process
✅ focuses on solution
✅ learns from mistake

---

### Scoring Rubric

| Score | Guide                                                    |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 5     | Owns issue + systematic troubleshooting + lesson learned |
| 3     | Fixed issue but limited reflection                       |
| 1     | Blames others / vague answer                             |

---

### Red Flags

❌ "Not my responsibility."

❌ Cannot explain troubleshooting steps.
